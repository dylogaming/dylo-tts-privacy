DYLO TTS Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 23, 2026

DYLO TTS ("the app") is a text to speech and voice cloning app made by DYLO Gaming. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: the app runs on your device and your voice recordings never leave your phone.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no sign in, and no analytics. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers. We do not have servers that receive your data.

Voice recordings and generated audio

When you clone a voice, the reference audio you provide is processed entirely on your device to create the voice. That audio, the voices you save, and the speech you generate are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Network use

The app connects to the internet for one purpose only: on first launch it downloads the voice model files it needs to run, from a HuggingFace content server. This download requests model files only and sends none of your personal data or recordings. After the models are downloaded, speech generation works on your device.

Data storage and deletion

All app data (downloaded models, saved voices, generated audio, and history) is stored locally on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Third parties and ads

The app contains no advertising, no third party analytics, and no tracking software.

Children

The app is a general purpose utility and is not directed at children.

Permissions

The app uses a foreground service so that speech generation can finish while the app is in the background, and internet access for the one time model download described above. No data leaves your device as a result of these permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact
