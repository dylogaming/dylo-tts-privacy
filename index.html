DYLO TTS Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

DYLO TTS ("the app") is a text to speech and voice cloning app made by DYLO Gaming. This policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: the app runs on your device and your voice recordings never leave your phone.

Data we collect

The app has no account and no sign in. It sends anonymous usage statistics to help us improve speed and stability:

  • A random install ID created by the app. It is not your device ID or advertising ID.
  • How long the app was open in a session.
  • For each finished generation: the audio length, how long it took, and the voice mode used.
  • The app version and your phone model.

We never collect your text, recordings, generated audio, name, email, or location. This data is not linked to your identity, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties. It is sent over an encrypted connection and stored on our own server, hosted by Cloudflare.

Voice recordings and generated audio

When you clone a voice, the reference audio you provide is processed entirely on your device to create the voice. That audio, the voices you save, and the speech you generate are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party.

Network use

The app connects to the internet to download the voice model files and app interface updates from a HuggingFace content server, and to send the anonymous usage statistics described above. Downloads send none of your personal data or recordings. Speech generation works on your device.

Data storage and deletion

All app data (downloaded models, saved voices, generated audio, and history) is stored locally on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Usage statistics are anonymous, so we cannot match them to you. To ask about them, email us at the address below.

Third parties and ads

The app contains no advertising, no third party analytics, and no tracking software.

Children

The app is a general purpose utility and is not directed at children.

Permissions

The app uses a foreground service so that speech generation can finish while the app is in the background, and internet access for the downloads and usage statistics described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to dylogamingofficial@gmail.com.
